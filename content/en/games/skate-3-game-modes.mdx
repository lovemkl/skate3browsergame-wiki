## Skate 3's Three Online Modes

Skate 3 launched in 2010 with three team-oriented online modes, and all three reward crew coordination over individual highlight reels. None appear offline — the disc has no multiplayer without community workarounds, which is why the browser port's online scene is such a big deal for returning players.

## 1-Up

The highlight-reel duel: teams take turns setting a trick line, and the opposing team has to beat the score. It's the purest expression of Skate's video culture — every run is a direct challenge to the last. Best with a crew that's honest about skill gaps; brutal with one.

## Domination

Territory control on skates: teams claim spots across the map by posting scores at them, and holding more spots at the timer wins. Domination rewards map knowledge and positioning over raw trick difficulty — the mode where beginners can carry a round by holding an easy spot while the pros duel.

## Own The Lot

The campaign-style mode: a series of challenges across Port Carverton, effectively the game's structured progression for online crews. Own The Lot is where most crews learn the maps before moving to free-form 1-Up sessions.

## Which Mode Suits Your Crew

| Crew type | Mode | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mixed skill levels | Domination | Beginners hold spots, pros score |
| High skill, competitive | 1-Up | Direct score duels, no hiding |
| New to the game together | Own The Lot | Structured progression, built-in tutorial flow |

## The Offline Reality

Skate 3 shipped with no offline multiplayer on the disc — the online modes require connection, and with official servers long past their prime, the community keeps them alive through the browser port's multiplayer and private lobbies. The [browser walkthrough](/guide/how-to-play-in-browser) covers the modern way in.

## FAQ

### Does Skate 3 have online multiplayer in 2026?

Through unofficial means, yes — the browser port and private lobbies keep the scene alive. The disc itself shipped with online modes but the official infrastructure is long gone.

### Which Skate 3 mode is best for beginners?

Domination — holding spots contributes without requiring pro-level trick scores, and beginners learn the maps while contributing.

### Is Own The Lot the story mode?

Close — it's the structured challenge progression for the online experience, not a narrative campaign. Skate 3's "career" is the offline Hall of Meat and career challenges.
