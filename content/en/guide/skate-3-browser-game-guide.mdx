## What the Port Actually Is

Precision matters with a claim this unlikely, so here is exactly what the Skate 3 Browser Game is and is not. It is the full Skate 3 game — Port Carverton, the career and challenges, the trick engine, online-era content, everything the disc contained — executed by the real game engine, delivered through a browser. It is not a remake, a demake, a Flash homage or a lookalike: the code running is the PlayStation 3 game's code, interpreted by RPCS3 compiled to run inside a Chrome-compatible browser environment.

That distinction — engine in the browser versus video over the wire — is the port's defining trick, and the technology page unpacks it fully. Streaming services like Xbox Cloud send you a movie of a game running elsewhere; your inputs travel up, frames travel back, and your device does nothing but decode video. The Skate 3 Browser Game flips that: the computation happens on your machine, inside the browser, through WebAssembly-compiled emulation. No datacenter bills, no server queues, no ownership of the game implied — just a browser tab doing, in 2026, what a $300 console did in 2010.
