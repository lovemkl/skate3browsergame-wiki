Not every session needs the full simulation. Three lighter routes cover the gaps:

## How the Round Loop Works

Every version above runs on the same engine of tension — the classic round loop, and it is worth learning once because it transfers to all of them:

1. **Earn points.** Every hit and every kill pays. Early rounds are cheap and fast; the points are your economy.
2. **Wall buy.** The chalk-outline weapons on walls are your first real guns — cheap, reliable, available every round. Buy early, not late.
3. **Mystery Box.** The box is the lottery: pay, roll, take your chances at a rare weapon. It is the mode's heart and its trap — the box does not owe you anything.
4. **Open Perk machines.** Perks are the survivability layer — speed, health, reload. A round-ten player with Perks beats a round-ten player without them every time.
5. **Board the windows.** Between waves, rebuild barriers. Boarding buys seconds, and seconds are the only currency the later rounds respect.

The loop repeats each round at a higher price: zombies get faster and more numerous, points come slower relative to need, and the run ends where your economy stops scaling. Master the loop in one browser port and you have mastered it in all of them — the controls differ, the grammar does not. For the input side of that equation, the [controls and tricks guide](/guide/controls-and-tricks) covers the keyboard-and-mouse habits worth building.

## Fan Ports and the Official Games: Know the Difference

The honest footnote: everything on this page is fan work. Nazi Zombies: Portable, the Black Ops 1 map ports, the demakes — none of it is official Activision content, and none of it replaces the real thing. The official Call of Duty titles, bought on Steam or played through Warzone's ecosystem, carry the licensed modes, the current maps, the progression systems and the servers. The browser ports are the community's love letter — free, instant, and legally gray at the edges in the way all fan ports are. Treat the browser scene as the free front door and the official games as the house; plenty of players use both.
